# VANTA — CP6-B Deep Environment Refinement

## Purpose

CP6-B develops the Phase 6 deep environment beyond its initial structural foundation. The environment now reads as a sequence of designed architectural depth layers rather than a repeated tunnel.

## Changes

- Added recessed side bays between the major structural frames.
- Added progressive ceiling baffles to create a measured architectural rhythm.
- Added a quiet far chamber with a framed wall, vertical reveal, and low platform.
- Added a second deep-environment light that remains extremely restrained and becomes relevant only near the far end of the journey.
- Preserved the existing material system, R3F architecture, native scroll system, mobile simplification, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Added no dependencies.

## Design intent

The deep environment should communicate that VANTA continues into a larger architectural complex. It deliberately avoids a sci-fi tunnel treatment or a glowing portal at the end.

This checkpoint was source-reviewed and structurally checked in the working environment. Full TypeScript, lint, production build, and browser verification remain part of the user's final testing pass, per the project workflow.
